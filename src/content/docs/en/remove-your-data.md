---
title: Remove your data
lede: One button removes everything Komorebi keeps on this PC.
description: Remove every password, cache file and setting Komorebi stored on your PC, before you uninstall or hand the PC on.
---

1. **Open Settings > Privacy.**
2. **Press Remove all data.** Confirm when Komorebi asks.
3. **Wait.** Komorebi deletes every saved password from Credential Manager, stops syncing and sending, and deletes the cache and its files.
4. **Uninstall, if you like.** Windows Settings > Apps removes the program too.

Mail on your servers is not touched. If a password cannot be deleted, Komorebi stops there and removes no files, so you can try again.

To remove one account only, use Settings > Accounts > the account > **Remove**.
