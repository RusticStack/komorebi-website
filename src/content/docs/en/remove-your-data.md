---
title: Remove your data
lede: One button removes everything Komorebi keeps on this PC.
description: Remove every password, cache file and setting Komorebi stored on your PC, before you uninstall or hand the PC on.
---

1. Open **Settings > Privacy**.
2. Press **Remove all data** and confirm.

Komorebi deletes every saved password from Credential Manager, then everything in its data folder: mail, drafts, the Outbox, settings and logs. Then it closes. Windows Settings > Apps removes the program too.

> **Your servers are not touched.** Mail on them stays. If a password cannot be deleted, Komorebi stops there and removes no files, so you can try again.

To remove one account only, use **Settings > Accounts**, open the account and press **Remove**.
