title: Use the calendar
lede: Month, week, day and agenda, beside your mail.
description: Use Komorebi's calendar: views, events, meeting invitations, CalDAV calendars and reminders.
---

1. **Open Calendar.** It is in the navigation, above Settings, and opens in the view you used last.
2. **Move around.** Use **Today**, <kbd>Page Up</kbd> and <kbd>Page Down</kbd>; <kbd>Home</kbd> goes back to today.
3. **Add an event.** **New event** asks for the title, time, repeat, reminder, place and notes. For a repeating event, choose **Only this event** or **The whole series**.

## Invitations

An invitation shows a card above the message. **Accept**, **Tentative** or **Decline** replies only to the organizer, or choose **Don't send a reply**. Accepted events join the local calendar, "On this PC".

## CalDAV calendars

**Calendars** on the command bar lists each account's calendars after you press **Find calendars**. They sync when the page opens and at most every 15 minutes while Komorebi runs.

## Reminders

Turn on **Notifications for event reminders** in Settings > General. They show only while Komorebi is open or in the notification area.
