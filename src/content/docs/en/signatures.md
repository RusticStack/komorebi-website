---
title: Use signatures
lede: Sign your mail once, and never type it again.
description: Create named signatures in Komorebi, choose a default per account for new mail and replies, and switch signatures while you write.
---

1. Open **Settings > Signatures** and press **Add signature**.
2. Type a name and write the signature, then press **Save**.
3. Open **Settings > Accounts**, choose your account, and set **Signature for new messages** and **Signature for replies and forwards**.

In the composer, the **Signature** menu swaps it; <kbd>Ctrl+Z</kbd> puts the old one back.

<details>
<summary>Good to know</summary>

- A signature is formatted text with at most one small logo, a PNG or JPEG up to 200 KB.
- Either default can be **None**.
- The signature goes under your text and above any quoted text.
- Choosing another **From** account swaps in that account's default while you have not changed it.

</details>
