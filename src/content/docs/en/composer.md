---
title: Write and send mail
lede: Formatted or plain text, with a few seconds to change your mind.
description: Write formatted or plain-text mail in Komorebi, add recipients and attachments, and undo a send.
---

1. **Start a message.** **New mail** <kbd>Ctrl+N</kbd>, **Reply** <kbd>Ctrl+R</kbd>, **Reply all** <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+R</kbd> or **Forward** <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+F</kbd>.
2. **Add recipients.** Addresses become chips after a comma, a semicolon or <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Suggestions come from your contacts first. **Cc** and **Bcc** are at the end of the To field.
3. **Write.** The toolbar has fonts, sizes, colours, lists, links, quotes and simple tables. A message with no formatting goes out as plain text.
4. **Attach files.** Use <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+A</kbd>, drop them on the window, or paste files copied in File Explorer. Up to 25 MB in all.
5. **Send.** Press <kbd>Ctrl+Enter</kbd>. The message waits in the Outbox for 5 seconds with **Undo**; Settings > General sets the delay, from off to 30 seconds.

Before it goes, a bar asks once when the subject is empty or your text mentions an attachment that is not there. **Send anyway** sends it as it is.

## Drafts

A draft is saved on this PC two seconds after you stop typing, and copied to the server's Drafts folder at most every 30 seconds. <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the composer and keeps the draft.

## If a message does not go

A message that could not reach the server stays in the Outbox and is tried again by itself, up to six times. One the server refused is marked failed, with the reason, **Retry** and **Edit**.
