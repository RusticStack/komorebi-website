Komorebi has no telemetry to turn off. It never had any. There is no Komorebi server to send data to.

## What it never sends

- Usage statistics or analytics.
- Crash reports.
- Your address book, mail or settings to anyone but your own servers.

## What it does connect to

| To | When |
|---|---|
| Your mail servers (IMAP, SMTP) | While it runs, over TLS or STARTTLS |
| Your CardDAV and CalDAV servers | Only if you set them up |
| Your domain's DNS and autoconfig | Only when you ask it to find settings |
| A message's image hosts | Only after you load that message's images |
| GitHub | Only for update checks and downloads |

The full list is in [What leaves your computer](/docs/privacy-and-network).

## Also private by design

- Remote images are blocked until you choose. Tracking pixels never load.
- Passwords stay in Windows Credential Manager.
- The mail cache can be [encrypted](/docs/encrypted-cache).
- This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.
