Komorebi is a desktop email client for Windows 10 and 11. It reads and writes IMAP mail, and does that one job without getting in the way.

<Shot name="inbox-personal" alt="Komorebi with a personal inbox open, a message in the reading pane" />

## What it does

- Reads and writes mail over IMAP and SMTP, with a local cache.
- Writes formatted mail with signatures, attachments and drafts.
- Keeps contacts and a calendar, synced over CardDAV and CalDAV if you want.
- Sorts new mail with local rules.
- Speaks English and European Portuguese.

## What it leaves out

| Left out | Why |
|---|---|
| AI features | A non-goal of the project |
| Telemetry | Nothing is sent to the project, ever |
| A web engine | HTML mail is drawn by Komorebi's own layout engine |
| A cloud account | Your mail goes only to your own mail servers |

## How it is built

- Written in Rust, with the Slint toolkit in its Fluent style.
- One program file under 20 MB. The installer is about 9 MB.
- Written budgets: a window in under 200 ms, a cached folder in under 600 ms.

## The name

Komorebi is Japanese for sunlight filtering through leaves.

## Who makes it

Komorebi is made by Mário Silva and published by [RusticStack](https://rusticstack.com). It is free and open source under GPL-3.0-or-later. The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/RusticStack/komorebi).
