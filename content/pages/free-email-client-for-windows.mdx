Komorebi is free in every sense: no price, no ads, no paid tier, and open source code under GPL-3.0-or-later.

<Shot name="inbox" alt="Komorebi in the light theme: the folder pane, the inbox and a message in the reading pane" />

## What free means here

| | Komorebi |
|---|---|
| Price | Free, with no paid tier |
| Ads | None |
| Account limit | None |
| Sign-up | No Komorebi account |
| Source | Open, GPL-3.0-or-later |

## What you get

- Several IMAP accounts and a combined inbox.
- A rich-text composer with signatures.
- Contacts and a calendar, with CardDAV and CalDAV.
- Local rules, with Copy as Sieve.
- English and European Portuguese.

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11 (x64).
- A mail account with IMAP and SMTP and a password or app password.

<Callout title="No OAuth">
Accounts that require OAuth sign-in, such as Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com, do not work.
</Callout>
