## All inboxes

Each account has its own folders. **All inboxes** combines every inbox. With more than one account, a colour dot marks each account's mail.

## Change an account

Settings > Accounts shows a card per account. Open it to change:

- the name, password and signatures;
- folders and server settings;
- whether it syncs, and **Download again**.

Leave the password box empty to keep the saved one.

## Remove an account

**Remove** asks first. Mail on the server stays. Drafts and Outbox messages of that account are lost, and the prompt says how many.
