Settings > **Rules** holds an ordered list of rules per account. They act on new Inbox mail, on this PC, while Komorebi runs.

<Shot name="rules" alt="Settings, Rules: four mail rules for the account, one paused, with recent activity" />

## Make a rule

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick the conditions">

Sender, To or Cc, subject, or has an attachment. Text matches by **contains**, **does not contain**, **is**, **starts with** or **ends with**, ignoring case and accents.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose all or any">

The rule matches when all conditions match, or any of them.

</Step>
<Step title="Pick the actions">

Move to a folder, delete (to Trash), mark read, mark important, or **Stop** (no later rule runs).

</Step>
</Steps>

<Shot name="rule-edit" alt="Editing a rule: its conditions and actions" />

## What rules never do

- Rules never send mail. No forward, redirect or auto-reply.
- They stay on this PC. The server only sees the moves and flags they make.

## When they run

Before a new message is stored. Mail a rule files away raises no notification. Mail that arrived while Komorebi was closed is sorted at the next start. Older mail is never touched.

## Copy as Sieve

**Copy as Sieve** puts your rules on the clipboard as a Sieve script. Paste it into your provider's filters to sort mail while Komorebi is closed. Nothing is sent.

<Advanced title="Activity and limits">

- The activity list shows each automatic run: time, rule, sender, subject and action. It keeps 500 lines per account. **Clear activity** empties it.
- A rule whose folder is gone pauses until you pick another.
- Running rules on an existing folder is not available yet.

</Advanced>
