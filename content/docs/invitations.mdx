## Answer an invitation

A message with an invitation shows a card above the body: title, time in your time zone, organiser, place and repeat.

<Shot name="invite" alt="A meeting invitation open, with its Accept, Tentative and Decline card" />

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| **Accept**, **Tentative** | Adds the event to your local calendar and replies |
| **Decline** | Removes it and replies |
| **Join** | Opens the meeting link, after the usual link prompt |
| **Save .ics** | Saves the calendar file |

The reply goes only to the organiser, never to Cc or other attendees. **Don't send a reply** keeps your answer on this PC.

## Reminders

The agenda marks an event under way ("Now") and one whose reminder is due ("In 10 minutes").

For a Windows notification too, turn on Settings > General > **Notifications for event reminders**. It shows the title, time and place, never the notes.
