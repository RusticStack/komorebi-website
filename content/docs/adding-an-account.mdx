---
title: Add an account
description: Add a mail account in three short steps. Built-in settings for Purelymail and Postale.io, lookup for other domains.
---

Open the steps from the welcome screen, or from Settings > Accounts > **Add account**.

<Shot name="onboarding-found" alt="Adding an account: a Purelymail address recognised, with its settings built in" />

<Steps>
<Step title="Type your email">

Press **Continue**. Purelymail and Postale.io addresses have their settings built in. For other domains, Komorebi looks the settings up.

</Step>
<Step title="Sign in">

Type your password, or an app password if your provider uses two-step sign-in. It is saved in Windows Credential Manager.

</Step>
<Step title="Make it yours">

Optional: your display name and a signature. **Skip** leaves both empty.

</Step>
</Steps>

Then "You're all set" shows the first download. Press **Open your inbox**.

## If sign-in fails

The message says what went wrong in one sentence, with a button for the next step:

- **Set username**: your user name is not your address.
- **Review certificate**: the server uses a self-signed or private certificate. See [certificate pinning](/docs/certificate-pinning).
- **Try again** or **Set up manually**: check the server settings.

**Show details** lists every step and server name.

<Callout type="tip" title="Your own domain">
Hosted at Purelymail or Postale.io? Choose that provider when Komorebi asks.
</Callout>
