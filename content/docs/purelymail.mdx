---
title: Set up Purelymail in Komorebi
nav: Purelymail
description: Add a Purelymail account to Komorebi, with a purelymail.com address or your own domain, in under a minute.
---

Komorebi has Purelymail's server settings built in. You only need your address and password.

<Steps>
<Step title="Start adding an account">

Settings > Accounts > **Add account**, or the welcome screen on the first start.

</Step>
<Step title="Type your Purelymail address">

Press **Continue**. A `purelymail.com` address is recognised at once.

</Step>
<Step title="Your own domain?">

If Komorebi asks for the provider, choose **Purelymail**.

</Step>
<Step title="Type your password">

Press **Sign in**. Komorebi checks the connection, the password and sending.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- **Save a copy to Sent** is on. Purelymail's documentation does not say whether the server saves sent mail itself, so Komorebi keeps a copy.
- Passwords stay in Windows Credential Manager.
- Purelymail and Komorebi are separate projects. Purelymail's own help is at [purelymail.com](https://purelymail.com).
