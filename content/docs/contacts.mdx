**Contacts** in the navigation is a local address book: names, addresses, phones, organisation, job title, notes and groups.

<Shot name="contacts" alt="The Contacts view with a contact card open" />

- Search the list, or filter by group.
- **New message** writes to the contact.
- <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> in the Name box saves.

The composer suggests your contacts first when you type a recipient.

## Sync with CardDAV

Sync is off until you turn it on, per account, in Contacts > **Sync**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose the mode">

**Read only** never writes to the server. **Read and write** sends your changes.

</Step>
<Step title="Find the server">

**Find** looks it up from your mail domain, without credentials. Or type the address.

</Step>
<Step title="Sign in">

**Find address books** signs in with your mail password, or turn off **Same as the mail password** to use another.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Advanced title="When it syncs and how conflicts are handled">

- Sync runs when Contacts opens, on **Sync now**, after an edit, and at most every 30 minutes while Komorebi is open.
- HTTPS only. Credentials go only to that address's server, never through a redirect elsewhere.
- Lookup: DNS `_carddavs._tcp`, then `https://<your domain>/.well-known/carddav`.
- When the same field changed on both sides, the server's value is kept and yours waits for your choice.
- Turning sync off never changes the server.

</Advanced>
