## Drafts

- Saved on this PC two seconds after you stop typing, and when the composer closes.
- Copied to the server's Drafts folder at most every 30 seconds. Each copy replaces the last.
- **Drafts** in the navigation lists them.

A draft from another device opens read only. Press **Edit** to continue it here. **Discard** asks first and removes the server copy too.

## Undo send

**Send** puts the message in the Outbox for 5 seconds, with **Undo**. Change the delay in Settings > General: off, 5, 10, 20 or 30 seconds.

A copy of exactly what was sent goes to Sent.

## Before you send

A bar asks once when:

- the subject is empty, or
- your text mentions an attachment but none is attached.

## When sending fails

| What happened | What Komorebi does |
|---|---|
| No connection, or "try later" | Keeps it in the Outbox and retries a few times |
| The server refused it | Marks it failed, says why, offers **Edit**, **Retry**, **Discard** |
| The connection broke mid-send | Marks it as maybe sent. Never sends it again by itself |
