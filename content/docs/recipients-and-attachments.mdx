## Recipients

Addresses in To, Cc and Bcc turn into chips when you type a comma, a semicolon or <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>.

- A wrong address is flagged before you send.
- Remove a chip with its **x** or <Kbd>Backspace</Kbd>.
- **Cc** and **Bcc** are links at the end of the To field.

## Suggestions

After two letters, up to six suggestions show: contacts first, then groups, then addresses from your mail. A group adds all its members.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| <Kbd>Up</Kbd> / <Kbd>Down</Kbd> | Pick a suggestion |
| <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> or <Kbd>Tab</Kbd> | Take it |
| <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> | Close the list |

## Attachments

Attach files in any of three ways:

1. <Kbd>Ctrl+Shift+A</Kbd> opens a dialog.
2. Drop files from File Explorer on the window.
3. Copy files in Explorer, then press <Kbd>Ctrl+V</Kbd> in the composer.

A line shows the total size, for example "Attachments 18.4 MB of 25 MB". It turns amber at 80% and red over 25 MB, when **Send** is off.
