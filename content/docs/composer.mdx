**New mail** (<Kbd>Ctrl+N</Kbd>), **Reply** (<Kbd>Ctrl+R</Kbd>), **Reply all** (<Kbd>Ctrl+Shift+R</Kbd>) and **Forward** (<Kbd>Ctrl+Shift+F</Kbd>) open the composer.

<Shot name="compose-table" alt="The composer with bold, italic, a colour, a link, a bulleted and a numbered list, a quote and a 3 by 3 table" />

## Formatting

The toolbar has:

- email-safe fonts and sizes;
- bold, italic, underline, strikethrough;
- ten named colours, or **Automatic** (works with the reader's dark mode);
- bulleted and numbered lists, indent, alignment, quotes;
- links (`http`, `https`, `mailto`) and tables up to 10 by 10.

Type `- `, `* ` or `1. ` at the start of a line to start a list.

## Plain text or formatted

| You write | Komorebi sends |
|---|---|
| No formatting | Plain text only, as typed |
| Any formatting | Plain text plus an HTML version |

**More** > **Switch to plain text** removes formatting. <Kbd>Ctrl+Z</Kbd> brings it back. **More** > **Preview** shows the message as the recipient gets it.

<Callout type="tip" title="Paste without formatting">
<Kbd>Ctrl+Shift+V</Kbd> pastes plain text.
</Callout>

<Advanced title="Defaults, importance and what is not supported">

- Settings > General > Compose sets whether new mail starts formatted, and its font, size and colour.
- **High importance** and **Low importance** set the standard `Importance` and `X-Priority` headers.
- Not supported: web fonts, a free colour picker, highlight, headings, images in the body and HTML source editing.

</Advanced>
