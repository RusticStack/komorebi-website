title: FAQ

---

<Faq q="Is Komorebi free?">
Yes. It is free and open source under GPL-3.0-or-later, with no ads, no paid tier and no account to create.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Which email providers work?">
Any provider with IMAP and SMTP and a password or app password, over TLS or STARTTLS. Purelymail and Postale.io have built-in settings. See [Add an account](/docs/adding-an-account).
</Faq>

<Faq q="Does it work with Gmail or Outlook.com?">
Komorebi has no OAuth sign-in. Gmail can work with an app password, if your Google account offers one. Accounts that require OAuth, such as Outlook.com and Microsoft 365, do not work.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Why does SmartScreen warn about the installer?">
The installer is not Authenticode-signed yet. Check its SHA-256 ([how](/docs/getting-started#check-the-download)), then choose More info and Run anyway. See the [code signing policy](/code-signing).
</Faq>

<Faq q="Does Komorebi send anything to its developers?">
No. There is no telemetry and no Komorebi server. The only request not to your own servers is the update check to GitHub. See [What leaves your computer](/docs/privacy-and-network).
</Faq>

<Faq q="Where are my passwords?">
In Windows Credential Manager, never in Komorebi's files.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I encrypt my mail on disk?">
Yes. Settings > Privacy > Encrypt the mail cache on this computer. See [Encrypted cache](/docs/encrypted-cache).
</Faq>

<Faq q="Which languages does it speak?">
English and European Portuguese. It follows the Windows display language, and Settings > General > Language can pin one.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Does it run on ARM, macOS or Linux?">
No. Releases are for Windows 10 and 11 on x64 only.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can it read S/MIME or PGP mail?">
No. Encrypted mail is shown as encrypted. You can save the encrypted part for a program that has the key.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Will it get AI features?">
No. AI features and telemetry are non-goals of the project.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Where do I report a problem?">
On [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/RusticStack/komorebi/issues). The optional diagnostics log (Settings > Privacy) holds no mail, so you can check it before you share it.
</Faq>
