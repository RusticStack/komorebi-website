---
title: Uninstall and remove data
nav: Uninstall
description: Uninstall Komorebi from Windows Settings, and remove its local data and saved passwords if you want.
---

<Steps>
<Step title="Remove your passwords first (optional)">

In Komorebi: Settings > Privacy > **Remove all data**. This deletes the saved passwords and every local file. The uninstaller never touches Credential Manager.

</Step>
<Step title="Uninstall">

Windows Settings > Apps > Komorebi > **Uninstall**.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose about your data">

The uninstaller asks whether to delete `%LOCALAPPDATA%\komorebi` too. The default is **No**.

</Step>
</Steps>

Mail on your servers is never changed.
