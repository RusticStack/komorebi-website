## Quick start [#quick-start]

<Steps>
<Step title="Install Komorebi">

Download the installer and run it. No admin rights needed. [Install Komorebi](/docs/getting-started)

</Step>
<Step title="Add your account">

Type your address and password. [Add an account](/docs/adding-an-account)

</Step>
<Step title="Send your first mail">

Press <Kbd>Ctrl+N</Kbd>, write, and press **Send**. [Write and format mail](/docs/composer)

</Step>
</Steps>
