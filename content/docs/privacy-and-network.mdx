Komorebi has no telemetry, ads or analytics, and uploads no crash reports. There is no Komorebi server.

## Every connection

| To | What | When |
|---|---|---|
| Your IMAP server | Sign-in, sync, flags, moves, drafts, Sent copies | While Komorebi runs |
| Your SMTP server | Mail you send, invitation replies | After Send and the undo delay |
| Your CardDAV and CalDAV servers | Contacts and events | Only if you set them up |
| Your DNS and your domain | Your mail domain only | Only when you press Continue, Find settings or Find |
| A message's image hosts | Image requests, no cookies or Referer | Only after you load that message's images |
| `api.github.com`, `github.com` | The update check and download | When you check, or once a day if automatic checks are on |

All mail connections use TLS or STARTTLS. Plaintext is refused.

## What stays on your PC

Everything is in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\komorebi`:

- `cache.sqlite3`: accounts, settings, mail, drafts, contacts, calendar and rules. Plaintext unless you turn on the [encrypted cache](/docs/encrypted-cache).
- `crash.log`: one line per crash, never mail text.
- `logs\komorebi.log`: only if you turn on the diagnostics log. Never addresses, subjects or text.

Passwords are only in Windows Credential Manager.

## Remove everything

Settings > Privacy > **Remove all data** deletes the saved passwords and every local file. Mail on your servers stays.

<Advanced title="More detail">

- `cache.drafts` holds copies of draft attachments; `cache.open` holds attachments you opened, removed at the next start.
- Remove all data stops syncing first. If a password cannot be deleted, it stops and removes no file.
- `KOMOREBI_OFFLINE=1`, a development switch, sends nothing at all.
- The full statement is on the [Privacy](/privacy) page.

</Advanced>
